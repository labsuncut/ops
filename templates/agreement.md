# Website agreement (simple)

Starting point only, not legal advice. Have someone qualified review it before relying on it.

---

**Between:** [our business name] ("we") and [client business name] ("you")
**Date:** [date]

1. **What you get.** A [package] website at [domain], built from the content you approve.
2. **Price.** [setup price] once, due before the site goes live on your domain. Then [monthly price] a month for hosting, security updates and up to [hours] of changes.
3. **Your content.** You own your business name, logo, photos and text. You confirm you have the right to use anything you send us.
4. **Your domain.** The domain is registered in your name. You keep it if you leave.
5. **Leaving.** Either side can stop the monthly plan with 30 days' notice. If you leave, we send you a copy of your site's files.
6. **Not included.** Paid ads, logos, photography, online stores and email accounts, unless agreed in writing.

Signed: ____________ (us) ____________ (you)
