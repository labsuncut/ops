# Pitch message

Fill the brackets. Keep it short; the link does the selling. Send it from your own phone or email, or show the site in person.

---

Hi [owner name or "there"],

I'm [your name], I build websites for local businesses in [town]. I noticed [business name] [doesn't have a website / has a site that doesn't work well on phones], so I made one to show you what it could look like:

[preview link]

It has your hours, a map, and a button customers can tap to call you. If you like it, it can be live on your own web address this week for [setup price] plus [monthly price] a month for hosting and changes.

Happy to change anything. Just reply or call me at [your phone].

[your name]

---

If emailing someone cold, add at the bottom: your mailing address and "Reply STOP and I won't email again."
